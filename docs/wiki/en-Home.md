# Unsuspicious Block

> An archaeology exploration and discovery mod for Minecraft 1.21.1.
>
> This page and this Wiki are based on release version `1.5.2`.

## What this mod does

Unsuspicious Block adds more complete exploration tools and documentation systems to Minecraft archaeology. You can scan suspicious blocks, quickly retrieve identified loot, and organize discovered loot tables, items, conditions, and locations in the archaeology journal.

## Features

- **Archaeology Journal**: A journal that records loot discovery locations and your exploration progress.
- **Suspicious Reader**: Scans the area for suspicious blocks or loot containers.
- **Archaeology Shovel**: Quickly clears sand/gravel to dig out suspicious blocks.
- **Lost Pages & Enchantments**: Discover four exploration- and collection-oriented enchantments through archaeology.
- **Eye of Cat**: Reveals otherwise hidden information in enchanting tables, anvils, and grindstones.
- **Specimen Box**: Carry five item slots, and let some items inside take effect while carried.
- **Pottery Wheel**: Duplicate pottery sherds, craft decorated pots, and supports hopper automation.
- **Unsuspicious Blocks**: Seal items inside sand or gravel disguised as suspicious blocks.
