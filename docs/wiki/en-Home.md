# Unsuspicious Block

> An archaeology exploration and discovery mod for Minecraft 1.21.1.
>
> This Wiki targets version `1.6.0` and is written for players.

## What This Mod Offers

Unsuspicious Block expands vanilla archaeology and treasure hunting with a complete set of field tools and collection mechanics: scan suspicious blocks before excavating, simulate drop odds across different setups in the Archaeology Journal, pan rivers and Nether lava, duplicate pottery sherds, ward off creepers and phantoms with Purrmint, and regenerate structures with Rewind Dust.

## Features

- **[Archaeology Journal](en-Archaeology-Journal)**: Browse categorized loot tables, simulate drop odds under custom conditions and luck, and log where you found every item.
- **[Suspicious Reader & Archaeology Shovel](en-Reader-and-Shovel)**: Scan and highlight suspicious blocks and unopened containers, view target details on your HUD, and extract scanned loot directly.
- **[Lost Pages and Enchantments](en-Lost-Pages-and-Enchantments)**: Smith Lost Pages into enchanted books carrying four enchantments for dredging, shearing, brushing, and fossil mining.
- **[Fishing and Panning](en-Fishing-and-Panning)**: Dredge shipwreck finds from open water, or use Copper, Gold, and Obsidian Pans at river and Nether lava spots.
- **[Eye of Cat](en-Eye-of-Cat)**: Carry it to reveal full enchanting table candidates, anvil cost breakdowns, and grindstone previews.
- **[Specimen Box](en-Specimen-Box)**: A five-slot portable container that keeps carried utilities, compatible artifacts, and Totems of Undying active inside.
- **[Pottery Wheel](en-Pottery-Wheel)**: Duplicate pottery sherds, craft custom four-sided pots with a 3D preview lightbox, and automate with hoppers.
- **[Unsuspicious Blocks](en-Unsuspicious-Blocks)**: Seal any item inside sand or gravel disguised as a suspicious block.
- **[Purrmint](en-Purrmint)**: An ancient plant found via Sniffers or river panning whose mature plants and potted forms repel Creepers and Phantoms through walls.
- **[Spark Dust and Rewind Dust](en-Spark-and-Rewind-Dust)**: Turn Torchflowers into furnace fuel, or craft Rewind Dust to regenerate world-generated structures in place.
