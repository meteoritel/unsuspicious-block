# Purrmint

Mature Purrmint can be placed as a plant or put into a vanilla flower pot. Both forms provide the same protective area.

## Obtaining and Growing

Sniffers can bring back Purrmint Seeds as an extra digging reward. Plant them on farmland and let them grow, or use bone meal to advance their growth. Bees carrying pollen can also help them grow. The crop becomes a separate mature plant, which drops itself when broken.

## Protective Area

The aura includes the source block and eight blocks in each direction along every axis, covering a `17×17×17` block cube. Walls do not block it. Overlapping auras form a combined protected area. Seedlings and empty pots provide no aura.

Creepers and phantoms avoid movement destinations and attack targets inside the area, and keep their active movement routes outside it. If a mob is already chasing you, its subsequent pathfinding or flight decision releases its target when it finds you inside, ending a natural swelling attack or swoop directed at you.

Natural phantom spawning skips players whose feet are inside the aura. Cat deterrence also provides independent spawning protection. Other players outside the area can still trigger phantom spawning.

Protection uses the block containing an entity's feet. Mobs already inside can leave on their own; the aura does not actively drive them out.

## Behavior and Limits

An aura source is active while its chunk is loaded. Removing the plant from its pot, breaking a source, or unloading its chunk removes its coverage. Other overlapping sources remain effective.

The aura constrains active movement. Vanilla inertia remains after propulsion stops, and gravity, knockback, and water currents can carry mobs into the area. Damage and explosions retain vanilla behavior, and creepers manually ignited with flint and steel or similar methods retain their vanilla ignition state.
